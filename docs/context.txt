6. LOCKED BUSINESS RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG RULES:
  F1: SGPA drop >= 1.0 vs previous semester
  F2: any subject mark < 40
  F3: SGPA < 6.0
  F4: effective internal < 40% OR internal avg drop >= 15 pts vs prev sem
  F5: upload deadline missed (two deadlines exist: internal + result)
  - First-semester students: only absolute rules (F2, F3); F1/F4 need baseline
  - Dedup: one UNRESOLVED flag per (rule, student, semester)
FLAG TRIGGER MAP (Q22-A):
  - Internal submission verified   -> evaluate F4 ONLY
  - Semester result verified       -> evaluate F1, F2, F3
  - Daily deadline job             -> F5 (reason records WHICH deadline missed)
LIFECYCLE: open -> in-discussion (meeting held) -> resolved (note MANDATORY)
           open 14 days -> ESCALATED -> admin force-resolve (note mandatory)
ALLOCATION: 1 active mentor per student; same dept enforced; reassign keeps
  history (end-date old record); capacity warning at 15
MEETINGS: slot statuses open/booked/completed/cancelled; reminders T-24h, T-1h;
  completing a meeting linked to open flag prompts resolution;
  meetings.slot_id MANDATORY (every individual meeting anchored to a slot)
SUBMISSIONS: pending -> verified (immutable, triggers engine) / rejected
  (note mandatory, student re-uploads; rejected attempts RETAINED for audit)
EFFECTIVE INTERNAL: best two of three tests, per subject; computed at
  verification; per-test max from GCE scheme (e.g. 20)

6a. DATABASE INTEGRITY STRATEGY (LOCKED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Defense in depth: app layer (friendly errors) + DB layer (hard guarantee).
Viva line: "A note on a diagram is a promise; a constraint is a guarantee."
DB-ENFORCED (partial unique indexes — SQL spec saved in docs/db-constraints.md,
  goes into Alembic migration Sprint 1. DBML cannot express WHERE clause —
  diagram shows plain unique + note; do NOT let Antigravity copy DBML index
  literally):
  - allocations: 1 active per student WHERE status='active'
  - internal_submissions: 1 live per (student,sem) WHERE status IN
    (pending,verified) — rejected unlimited
  - semester_results: same as above
  - flags: 1 unresolved per (rule,student,sem) WHERE status IN
    (open,in_discussion,escalated)
  - meetings: 1 non-cancelled per slot (partial)
PLAIN UNIQUES: internal_marks (submission_id,subject_name); result_subjects
  (result_id,subject_name); group_attendance (session_id,student_id)
APP-LAYER ONLY (correct answer, no DB constraint possible/practical):
  - verified-record immutability (mention DB trigger as enterprise option)
  - same-department allocation validation
  - capacity warning at 15
NOTE: SQLite AND PostgreSQL both support partial indexes — no portability issue.
FK deletes default to RESTRICT — correct, soft-delete only.
